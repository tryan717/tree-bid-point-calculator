Point Definitions

Tree Size Points

Small: 5 pts, Medium: 10 pts, Large: 16 pts, XL: 23 pts.

Canopy Points

Light: 0 pts, Medium: +1 pt, Large: +3 pts, XL: +6 pts.

Normal Rigging

+2 pts. Standard controlled lowering with ropes. Cannot be selected with Technical Rigging.

Technical Rigging

+5 pts. Complex rigging, negative blocking, limited tie-ins, or heavy pieces over targets. Cannot be selected with Normal Rigging.

Near Structure/Fence

+2 pts. Tree is near a house, fence, shed, driveway, or feature, but risk is manageable.

High-Risk Structure/Fence

+5 pts. Tree or major limbs are directly over a structure, or a mistake would likely cause expensive damage.

Dead/Brittle

+3 pts. Compromised wood, brittle canopy, hollow areas, or unsafe tie-in/rigging concerns.

Poor Drop Zone

+3 pts. Limited landing area, obstacles below, tight yard, landscaping, or other restrictions.

Bad Access / Long Drag

+2 pts. Difficult machine access, long drag distance, tight gate, or limited staging area.

Steep Terrain

+2 pts. Sloped or unstable terrain that slows cleanup, machine work, climbing, dragging, or hauling.

Heavy Cleanup / Wood Volume

+2 pts. Large amount of brush, limbs, debris, or cleanup time beyond a normal removal.

XL Logs / Oversized Wood

+3 pts. Large diameter wood, usually over 32 inches, requiring extra machine time, cutting, loading, or handling.
